Click Cookies Cookie Types Guide: All 21 Cookies & Rarity Tiers

By Waqas Bukhari • Reference • 10 min read

Every cookie you click a cookie for is worth a different amount depending on its rarity. This reference lays out all six tiers so you always know what to prioritize when you click the cookie.

⚡ Quick Answer

Click Cookies has 21 cookie types split across six rarity tiers — Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, and Mythic. Rarer cookies are worth dramatically more points, with the Mythic-tier Infinity Cookie topping the list at 1,000 points, but they only appear after you reach Level 10.

Rarity System Overview

Every cookie that falls in the arena belongs to one of six rarity tiers, and the tier determines both how often it appears and how many points it's worth when you click a cookie successfully. Lower tiers spawn frequently and form the backbone of your score, while higher tiers are rare but dramatically more rewarding.

Common & Uncommon Cookies

Common cookies, led by the Classic Cookie, are the ones you'll click the most by far. They're intentionally low-value so that Uncommon cookies feel like a meaningful step up. Don't ignore these tiers just because they're not flashy — they make up the bulk of your total score across any long run, especially before your combo multiplier kicks in.

Rare & Epic Cookies

Rare and Epic cookies are worth prioritizing when two cookies are falling near each other and you only have time to catch one. They're distinct enough visually — brighter colors, subtle glow effects — that with practice you'll spot them instinctively while you click a cookie mid-combo.

Legendary & Mythic Cookies

Legendary cookies are rare enough that catching one usually justifies breaking away from a nearby Common cookie. Mythic cookies — the Void Cookie (500 pts) and the Infinity Cookie (1,000 pts) — are the crown jewels of the game. They only start appearing once you reach Level 10, and even then their spawn weight is around 3%, so most players see them only a handful of times per session.

💡
Unlock Tip

The Golden Touch upgrade increases the spawn weight of Legendary and Rare cookies, which indirectly makes Mythic sightings feel more frequent too. It's worth saving for once you're consistently reaching Level 10.

How to Spot Rare Cookies Fast

Recognizing rarity at a glance is a skill that improves with playtime, but a few shortcuts help early on:

  • Rarer cookies tend to use brighter, more saturated colors than Common cookies.
  • Legendary and Mythic cookies often have a visible glow or particle effect around them.
  • If you're unsure whether a cookie is worth breaking your current path for, it's almost always worth catching — the point difference between tiers is significant.

If you're still getting comfortable with the basics of catching and combos, start with the beginner's guide before trying to optimize for rarity.

FAQs

How many cookie types are in Click Cookies? ▼
There are 21 unique cookie types across six rarity tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, and Mythic.
What is the highest value cookie? ▼
The Infinity Cookie is the highest value Mythic cookie at 1,000 points, followed closely by the Void Cookie at 500 points.
How do I unlock Mythic cookies? ▼
Mythic cookies unlock once you reach Level 10, then appear randomly with a low spawn weight. The Golden Touch upgrade helps increase how often rare and legendary cookies appear.
